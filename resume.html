About Me

I'm Dylan Kembre. I grew up in West Chester, Ohio, and now live in West Carrollton, just south of Dayton, with my wife Alyssa, our three boys, Simon (4), Cohen (2) and Mason (1), and Winston, our dopey dog.

During the day I'm a Project Coordinator at River Valley Credit Union, which serves the Miami Valley community. In the gym, I'm a varsity assistant at Springboro High School, where I focus on data analytics and player development. When I'm not doing either, I'm usually reading, watching the Bengals or the Reds, or hanging out with my family.

I love coaching volleyball, and practice is one of my favorite places to be. I enjoy challenging players, helping them develop, and paying attention to every small detail that makes them better. Most of you probably know me from private lessons more than from coaching a team, and I'm proud of the reputation I've built as a trainer.

I rarely talk about myself or my past, so this page puts my experience in volleyball and in life in one place. It isn't here to brag. It's just something you can read if you're curious.

Looking at Colleges?

One of my favorite things about this sport is the network it has built. Friends and former players of mine now coach at high schools, clubs and colleges all over the country, and I'm always learning from them.

If there's a college you're interested in, let me know. There's a good chance I have someone in my phone we can reach out to for honest, unbiased information about the program.
